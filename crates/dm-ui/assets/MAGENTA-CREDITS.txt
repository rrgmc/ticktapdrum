magenta-grooves.json holds 359 drum grooves, beats and fills, from the Groove MIDI Dataset.

Groove MIDI Dataset, by Google Magenta.
Jon Gillick, Adam Roberts, Jesse Engel, Douglas Eck and David Bamman. "Learning to Groove with
Inverse Sequence Transformations." International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2019.
https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove

Changes made for TickTapDrum: only the takes TickTapDrum can play were kept (in 4/4, on the
grid, at most 16 bars), grouped by style; the drummers' timing was moved onto a grid of 16 steps per bar, and
the Roland TD-11 drum keys were mapped to General MIDI. The choice is made by
crates/dm-groove/examples/curate_magenta.rs.
